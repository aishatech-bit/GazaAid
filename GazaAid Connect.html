How we check

Connecting donors to organisations they can trust

GazaAid Connect is independent and run by volunteers. We list only established humanitarian organisations working in Gaza and the wider Palestinian territories.

Listings are reviewed against UN OCHA partner information and charity regulator records, and we date-stamp each review.

Standards for every listing

  • Appears in UN OCHA partner registries or is a UN agency
  • Registered with a recognised charity regulator
  • Active operations confirmed in the field
  • Published financial reports
  • No political or military affiliations

Before you give anywhere else

  • Check the web address matches the organisation’s official site
  • Be wary of personal accounts and urgent private messages
  • Prefer card or bank payments over crypto or gift cards
